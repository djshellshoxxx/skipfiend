# SKIPFIEND, explained simply

SKIPFIEND is an audio effect that makes music sound as if playback is misbehaving. It can create skips, repeats, stutters, dropouts, and other deliberate “broken player” sounds from the audio passing through it.

It keeps a recent piece of incoming sound in memory and uses different playback tricks to repeat or interrupt parts of it. Rhythm, chance, a sequencer, sidechain input, or MIDI can influence when the effects happen.

It is a creative effect, not a repair tool. It changes the live sound passing through the plugin; it does not damage or rewrite the original audio file.
